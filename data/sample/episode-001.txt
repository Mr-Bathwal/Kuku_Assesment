EPISODE NUMBER: 1
EPISODE LOGLINE: On her last night shift before transferring wards, Nurse Priya Nair notices a dose on a patient's chart that she never administered, and realises someone has been writing in her handwriting.
CONTINUITY NOTES: Ward 4C, 02:00-03:00. Priya wears the navy scrubs with the chipped ID badge. Mr Khanna is unconscious throughout. Dr Rao is off-screen until the final beat.
